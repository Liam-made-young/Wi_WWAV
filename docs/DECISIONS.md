conversation are marked **Decided** in `docs/SPEC.md`; this file logs the ones
made while building, including every Open item built at its recommendation
before the founder answered (`docs/QUESTIONS.md`).

| 2026-10-07 | Space's ring stagger (QUESTIONS #72, first fix): each ring turns from iOS's half seat per ring to where its worlds keep the most room from the worlds of the rings inside it, counting room from the ring just inside once and room from rings further in twice (`ringTurn` in `app/ui/src/space/model/orbits.ts`). | 2026-10-07 | Worlds that come close are nudged apart judging distance on iOS's screen, with y squashed by its 0.88 view tilt (`separationOffsets`), though the model's plane is otherwise unsquashed and the camera supplies the tilt. | The nudge moves worlds, so measuring it in another frame would place the same system differently on the iPhone and the Mac. | Measuring in the 3D plane. |
| 2026-10-07 | A mute always silences, and soloing a stem un-mutes it. A click (or M) on a soloed stem mutes it and leaves the solo, so it is silent and the others stay silenced; its shape is 8.3's muted centre with the solo's outer ring, read "Vocals, soloed and muted". A double-click, or S, that solos a muted stem un-mutes it, and a double-click on a soloed stem clears the solo. | 4.6 says a click mutes at once, and the click has to be heard, seen and read. These rules give exactly iOS's sound, where a solo is every other stem muted: the same click leaves the same silence and a double-tap solos or clears as there. | A click that clears the solo (the other stems would come back, which iOS doesn't do); solo outranking mute (the click changes nothing). |
| 2026-10-07 | The stem gesture captures the first pointer down and ignores every other pointer until it lifts; a ⌘-click (Ctrl on Windows and Linux) on a stem is `enter` (5.8), never a mute, and Return is `enter` too; with ⌘ held, keys are the app's shortcuts (⌘S, ⌘M), never a stem's. A second click counts per stem, as iOS keeps one last tap per moon. | A second finger's lift ended the first finger's press; ⌘-click and ⌘S arrived as a mute and a solo. | Leaving pointers and modifiers to each surface. |
| 2026-10-07 | The Now strip's four lights stand in a row of 44 pt squares at the start of the track half's line 2 (x 260.5–436.5 of the 520 pt strip, 220.5–396.5 of the 440 pt one), each square the strip's full 44 pt height, leaving 83.5 pt of line 2 for "1:42 / 3:58" (43.5 pt in the narrow strip). | 2.2's 44 × 44 pt hit areas; squares spaced closer would share their overlap and each be less than 44 wide. | Squares centred on the lights, which would overlap and reach below the strip. |
